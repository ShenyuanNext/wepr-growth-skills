# Founder growth framework

Use this as a decision catalog, not a checklist to execute in full.

## Stage priorities

| Stage | Main uncertainty | Suitable work | Evidence to earn next |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–10 | Is the problem urgent? | interviews, manual outreach, concierge delivery, narrow communities | repeated problem language, meetings, commitments, willingness to pay |
| 10–100 | Is acquisition repeatable? | sharpen ICP/positioning, test 1–2 channels, build proof and onboarding | qualified conversion, activation, testimonials, early retention |
| 100–1,000 | Can the loop scale? | lifecycle, referrals, SEO/content systems, partnerships, measured paid tests | stable economics, retention, attributable pipeline, playbooks |

## Channel catalog

Evaluate launch surfaces and directories; founder networks, partnerships, communities, and events; social publishing and listening; founder-led sales and outbound; SEO and answer-engine/GEO discovery; community-native participation; email and lifecycle; educational content, distribution, lead magnets, and PR; paid media and sponsorships; creators; affiliates and referrals; free tools and engineering-led marketing; landing, comparison, proof, pricing, and conversion pages; packaging; validation, user research, and jobs-to-be-done interviews.

## Selection rules

1. Start where the ICP already expresses the problem or evaluates alternatives.
2. Prefer channels that produce learning as well as leads during early stages.
3. Confirm the team can sustain the cadence after launch.
4. Count asset reuse across sales, content, PR, search, and lifecycle.
5. Treat platform tactics and benchmarks as time-sensitive; verify current rules and data.
6. Avoid covert promotion, bulk spam, fake engagement, fabricated reviews, and manipulative urgency.

## Experiment contract

> We believe [audience] experiencing [trigger] will take [behavior] when offered [message/asset] through [channel], because [evidence]. We will test this with [budget/time/sample], stop if [threshold], and scale if [threshold] by [decision date].

Capture customer language, objections, perceived alternatives, proof requested, and reasons for non-conversion alongside numeric results.
