# Content Quality And Programmatic SEO

Use this module for content assessment, pruning, consolidation, originality, scaled publishing, and quality recovery. It does not assign an “E-E-A-T score” or infer ranking weights.

## Page-value test

Evaluate a page against its intended audience and task:

- satisfies the dominant intent and expected page type
- contributes first-hand experience, original data, analysis, tooling, examples, or a defensible point of view
- makes important claims verifiable through sources, authorship, methodology, or product evidence
- is current where price, availability, law, software, research, safety, or other facts materially change
- lets the user complete the task without padding, forced pagination, misleading UI, or conversion obstruction
- is distinct from the site's other pages and from commodity summaries already abundant on the web

Authorship and reputation can support trust, especially for consequential topics, but do not turn them into a numeric ranking-factor formula.

## Content decision ledger

Choose at page or template level:

| Decision | Use when | Required evidence |
|---|---|---|
| keep | useful and performing or strategically necessary | page role and current quality |
| improve | correct intent but incomplete, stale, weak, or poorly delivered | concrete content/user gap |
| consolidate | multiple pages solve materially the same task | query/page or SERP-overlap evidence plus redirect plan |
| reposition | page type or intent mismatches the opportunity | current SERP and business fit |
| remove | no user value, legal/safety reason, expired resource, or unrecoverable duplication | page inventory, dependencies, links, traffic, replacement decision |

Do not mass-delete pages solely because they have zero recent clicks. New, seasonal, niche, navigational, support, legal, or low-volume high-value pages can still be necessary. For destructive work, preserve the decision inventory and rollback path.

## Programmatic SEO launch gate

Template scale is justified only when all gates pass:

1. repeated user jobs and page patterns are real, not manufactured keyword permutations
2. every indexable page has page-specific value beyond swapped variables
3. data provenance, licensing, accuracy, freshness, and missing-value behavior are defined
4. templates expose crawlable URLs, useful navigation, correct status/canonical behavior, and stable rendering
5. empty, duplicate, impossible, and low-value combinations are prevented or intentionally excluded
6. a staged launch and holdout/sample review exists before full index exposure
7. monitoring covers coverage, quality, engagement, conversion, crawl behavior, and policy risk

The strength of a programmatic system is the usefulness and defensibility of its data and interaction, not URL count.

## AI-assisted content

The production method is not a quality verdict. Require the same usefulness, originality, factual review, disclosure/compliance where relevant, and maintenance ownership whether humans or AI tools assisted.

Reject:

- paraphrased competitor summaries presented as original expertise
- pages created for every fan-out or long-tail variation without distinct user value
- fake experience, reviews, authors, dates, citations, or entity mentions
- translated pages without market-specific review
- automated publishing without sampling, factual checks, and rollback
