# WEPR presentation delivery

## Choose Bento deliberately

Use Bento when the client benefits from a browser-openable, single-file,
interactive presentation with motion, charts, state slides, and embedded
speaker notes. Use PowerPoint when the client explicitly requires `.pptx`,
corporate template compatibility, or extensive Office-side editing.

## Build from approved material

1. Establish the source of truth: proposal, quotation workbook, research
   report, brief, or approved copy.
2. Create a slide evidence map with claim, source, date, confidence, and owner.
3. Keep budget totals, service units, platform names, periods, and exclusions
   identical across the deck and its source documents.
4. Label estimates, examples, platform samples, and verified facts distinctly.
5. Do not add rankings, market data, customer claims, or performance promises
   unless the source pack supports them.

## Recommended client narrative

1. Cover: client objective and period.
2. Executive answer: the recommended direction in one screen.
3. Context: audience, platform, business problem, and evidence boundary.
4. Diagnosis or opportunity map.
5. Strategy and workstreams.
6. Deliverables, cadence, owners, and acceptance criteria.
7. Measurement and attribution limits.
8. Timeline and dependencies.
9. Pricing and scope boundaries.
10. Decision page and next steps.

Use charts for real comparable numbers, tables for scope and pricing, morphs
for before/after or phased strategy, and state slides for optional detail.

## WEPR visual defaults

- Prefer a restrained editorial or consulting style.
- Use one accent color and no more than two typefaces.
- Keep the WEPR identity primary; do not add upstream project promotion.
- Keep each slide focused on one decision or message.
- Write concise speaker notes that explain evidence and caveats.
- Produce an editable working file and, when requested, a readonly handout.

## Acceptance check

- Open the `.bento.html` in a modern browser.
- Confirm the `#bento-doc` JSON parses and the runtime remains untouched.
- Check every slide at 1280×720 with 96 px side margins.
- Verify charts, tables, morph IDs, links, state slides, media, and notes.
- Confirm all figures against the source document.
- Check that confidential assets are embedded only with authorization.
- Confirm offline behavior when the deliverable is promised as self-contained.
