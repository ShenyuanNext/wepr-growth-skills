# Client PR plan

## Proposal architecture

1. **Executive decision:** recommended direction, why it matters now, and approval required.
2. **Business context:** action, objective, audience, market, timing, and constraints.
3. **Evidence state:** confirmed facts, reported claims, disputes, unknowns, and research gaps.
4. **Reputation diagnosis:** stakeholder expectations, trust conditions, issue state, and business exposure.
5. **Strategic choice:** communication role, priority audiences, narrative, channels, and deliberate exclusions.
6. **Workstreams:** research, message system, media, owned content, executive/spokesperson, stakeholder engagement, monitoring, and review.
7. **Delivery plan:** 30/60/90-day milestones, deliverables, owner/RACI, dependencies, approval SLAs, and budget.
8. **Measurement:** baselines, output quality, audience understanding, qualified coverage, inquiry behavior, search/narrative signals, and business proxy metrics.
9. **Scenario control:** triggers, escalation levels, holding materials, decision authority, and continuity.
10. **Commercial scope:** quantity, cadence, revision rounds, client inputs, exclusions, expenses, taxes, and payment terms.

## Strategy logic

For each workstream complete:

| Field | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Objective | Observable audience or stakeholder change |
| Evidence | Why this workstream is needed |
| Audience | Specific group and decision context |
| Message | Claim, support, boundary, next action |
| Deliverable | Countable artifact or activity |
| Owner | Accountable person and approver |
| Timing | Start, checkpoint, delivery date |
| KPI | Definition, source, baseline, target logic |
| Trigger | Condition to stop, revise, scale, or escalate |
| Risk | Operational, factual, legal, platform, or trust exposure |

## Measurement hierarchy

- **Readiness:** evidence completeness, spokesperson readiness, approval speed, response SLA.
- **Output quality:** factual accuracy, message consistency, correction rate, relevant media acceptance.
- **Audience signal:** recall, understanding, qualified engagement, question themes, stakeholder feedback.
- **Reputation behavior:** recommendation, inquiry quality, partner confidence, complaint resolution, search narrative.
- **Business contribution:** qualified pipeline, retention risk, conversion support, regulatory/partner continuity where attribution is credible.

Do not promise sentiment, coverage, rankings, sales, or crisis prevention without defensible assumptions and measurement boundaries.

## Client-facing acceptance test

The proposal must make clear what decision the client is buying, which facts and client inputs are required, what WEPR will deliver and not deliver, how approvals work, how success is measured, and what conditions change the plan.
